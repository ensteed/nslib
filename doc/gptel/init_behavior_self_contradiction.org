*** What =member{}= buys you
It guarantees that member gets value-initialized /whenever the object is actually constructed/, even if the enclosing object is not brace-initialized.

};

- What /doesn’t/ happen in =S b;= is implicit zeroing of members that have *no* default member initializer.

*** Why your earlier conclusion happened
